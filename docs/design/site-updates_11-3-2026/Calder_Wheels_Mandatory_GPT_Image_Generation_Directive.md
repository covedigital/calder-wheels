# CALDER WHEELS — MANDATORY IMAGE GENERATION DIRECTIVE

**Project:** Calder Wheels — Cinematic Website Hero Video  
**Image generation engine:** ChatGPT Image Generation  
**Downstream production:** Higgsfield Image-to-Video  
**Priority:** Reference fidelity, product accuracy, photorealism, and frame-to-frame consistency.

## 1. Absolute Reference Fidelity — Non-Negotiable

All supplied reference images must be treated as the authoritative visual source.

This is a commercial automotive product showcase, **not** a conceptual vehicle redesign.

You must preserve the appearance, proportions, geometry, and identifying characteristics of the reference vehicle and Calder wheel designs as faithfully as possible.

Do not creatively reinterpret, redesign, simplify, approximate, or substitute vehicle or wheel components.

**The Calder wheel design is the primary commercial product. Its visual accuracy takes precedence over cinematic styling.**

## 2. Vehicle Design Lock

Preserve the following characteristics as shown in the reference images:

- Vehicle make, model, generation, and body style.
- Original body proportions, wheelbase, and ride height.
- Hood, grille, bumpers, headlights, and taillights.
- Window shapes, pillars, mirrors, and roofline.
- Door handles, badges, trim, and running boards.
- Original paint color and finish.
- Wheel arch geometry and tire positioning.
- Vehicle-specific details and accessories.

Do not invent, remove, reposition, or modify identifying vehicle features. When generating a different camera angle, reconstruct only what is necessary to achieve the requested perspective, using the provided references. Do not introduce unsupported details.

## 3. Calder Wheel Design Lock — Highest Priority

Every Calder wheel must match the supplied approved wheel reference as closely as possible.

Preserve:

- Exact spoke count and arrangement.
- Spoke thickness, curvature, spacing, and orientation.
- Center disk geometry.
- Center cap shape and Calder branding.
- Outer barrel and lip construction.
- Hardware and bolt count, positioning, and finish.
- Depth, concavity, and offset appearance.
- Approved material colors and surface finishes.
- Overall wheel-to-tire proportions.

**Never replace an approved Calder wheel with a visually similar generic aftermarket wheel.**

Do not invent additional spokes, bolts, decorative elements, or alternative finishes. Both visible wheels must represent the same approved design and configuration unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

If an approved wheel reference cannot be reproduced accurately, do not silently substitute another design. Identify the limitation.

## 4. Camera Angle and Composition

Follow the requested camera angle and vehicle orientation precisely. Examples:

- Front three-quarter facing right.
- Front three-quarter facing left.
- Rear three-quarter facing right.
- Rear three-quarter facing left.
- Direct side profile.
- Low-angle wheel detail.
- Close-up product hero shot.

Ensure realistic perspective, correct wheel alignment, consistent vehicle proportions, and physically plausible camera positioning.

Do not mirror or reverse the reference image if that would incorrectly reverse vehicle-specific details. Maintain sufficient framing around the vehicle to support subsequent camera movement in Higgsfield. Avoid unnecessary cropping of wheels, bumpers, or roofline.

## 5. Photorealism — No AI Artifacts

The image must resemble a professionally photographed, high-budget automotive advertisement.

Required characteristics:

- Physically plausible lighting and shadows.
- Accurate automotive paint reflections.
- Realistic glass, rubber, metal, and chrome materials.
- Natural tire-to-ground contact.
- Consistent perspective and scale.
- Realistic depth of field.
- Detailed but natural surface textures.
- Controlled cinematic contrast.

Strictly prohibit:

- Warped or melted vehicle panels.
- Deformed wheels or incorrect spoke patterns.
- Floating tires or unrealistic suspension.
- Inconsistent headlights or grille geometry.
- Fake, unreadable, or distorted branding.
- Duplicate components.
- Unrealistic reflections.
- Excessive sharpening, artificial gloss, or CGI-like surfaces.
- Unrequested visual embellishments.
- AI-generated visual artifacts or hallucinated details.

## 6. Higgsfield Animation Readiness

Every generated image must be suitable for use as an image-to-video keyframe.

Prioritize:

- Clean, stable vehicle geometry.
- Clearly defined wheel details.
- Realistic separation between foreground and background.
- Consistent lighting direction.
- Adequate negative space for camera movement.
- Natural reflections that can be animated without obvious distortion.
- Stable vehicle placement and ground contact.

Avoid excessive motion blur, extreme perspective distortion, artificial speed effects, or visual elements likely to deform during animation. Unless explicitly requested, the vehicle must appear stationary.

## 7. Continuity Between Generated Assets

When producing multiple images of the same vehicle, maintain visual continuity across every asset:

- Vehicle identity and model.
- Vehicle paint color.
- Calder wheel design and finish.
- Tire appearance.
- Vehicle modifications and accessories.
- Environmental setting when part of the same scene.
- Time of day and lighting conditions.
- Overall photographic treatment.

Do not change vehicle features between angles or shots.

## 8. Reference Priority

When multiple reference images are supplied, use this hierarchy:

1. **Approved Calder wheel product rendering:** authoritative for wheel geometry and finishes.
2. **Original vehicle reference:** authoritative for vehicle identity, body geometry, and accessories.
3. **Additional vehicle angles:** supporting evidence for hidden surfaces and perspective.
4. **Approved environment reference:** authoritative for background and lighting.
5. **Written shot instructions:** authoritative for requested composition and camera direction.

If references conflict, preserve the higher-priority source and flag the conflict rather than inventing a compromise.

## 9. Generation Quality Control

Before finalizing an image, evaluate:

- Does the vehicle match the reference?
- Are the Calder wheels the correct design?
- Are spoke geometry, center caps, and hardware consistent?
- Are visible wheels proportionally correct?
- Are body panels, windows, and lighting physically plausible?
- Does the requested camera angle match?
- Is the image suitable for Higgsfield animation?
- Are there any invented or distorted details?

Do not claim exact fidelity unless it can be verified against the supplied references. If accuracy is uncertain, explicitly identify the affected detail.

## 10. Final Directive

**REFERENCE FIDELITY OVERRIDES CREATIVE INTERPRETATION.**

Do not prioritize dramatic aesthetics over product accuracy. Do not hallucinate missing components. Do not replace Calder wheel designs with generic alternatives. Do not modify the vehicle beyond explicitly requested changes.

Generate a realistic, premium, production-ready automotive image that preserves the approved Calder Wheels product design as faithfully as possible.

**This directive applies to every image generated for the Calder Wheels hero video unless explicitly overridden by the user.**
