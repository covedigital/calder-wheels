05 // STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

ENGINEERING SERIES.

Three distinct metallurgical processes engineered for specific motorsport classes, torque profiles, and vehicular dynamics.

TIER 01 ULTRA-HIGH PERFORMANCE

FORGED MONOBLOCK

Carved exclusively from 10,000-ton forged 6061-T6 aerospace aluminum billets. Monolithic grain flow yields unmatched tensile strength, near-zero porosity, and maximum un-sprung mass reduction.

MATERIAL: 6061-T6 BILLET
GRAIN STRUCTURE: CONTINUOUS RADIAL
IDEAL USE: HYPERCAR / GT3 CUP
TIER 02 TRACK & ROAD

HYBRID FLOW-FORMED

Cast wheel centers dynamically heated and spun while high-pressure hydraulic rollers compress and elongate the barrel. Yields forged-like density along the rim band for severe curb impact absorption.

MATERIAL: FLOW-ELONGATED A356
SHOCK ABSORPTION: +35% VS CAST
IDEAL USE: SPORT SEDANS / DAILY PERFORMANCE
TIER 03 BESPOKE TAILORED

3-PIECE MODULAR

Engineered with forged centers fastened to precision-spun inner barrels and stepped outer lips using titanium hardware. Allows sub-millimeter custom offsets and aggressive outer dish depths for widebody builds.

FASTENERS: TITANIUM M8 TORX
OFFSET RANGE: ET -40MM TO +65MM
IDEAL USE: EXOTIC COMMISSIONS / WIDEBODY
06 // CALDER PROTOCOL

THE PROCESS.

SIX STEPS FROM RAW BILLET TO PRECISION FITMENT
01
SPECIFY & MEASURE

Laser scanning hub, scrub radius, and caliper envelopes down to 0.05mm precision.

02
CAD & FEA SIM

Finite element stress analysis simulating curb impacts and 2.5G cornering loads.

03
CNC MILLING

Multi-axis robotic milling carve spoke profiles and back-pad pocketing for un-sprung mass.

04
HAND FINISHING

Hand brushwork followed by electro-deposited ceramic powder or PVD titanium coat.

05
AEROSPACE QC

X-ray density inspection, dynamic acoustic runout balancing, and TÜV spec sign-off.

06
BESPOKE CRATING

Hermetically sealed in tailored wooden flight crates with custom serial certification plaque.
