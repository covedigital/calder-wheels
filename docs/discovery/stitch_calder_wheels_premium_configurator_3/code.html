§ 05 // Structural integrity

Engineering series.

Three distinct metallurgical processes engineered for specific motorsport classes, torque profiles, and vehicular dynamics.

TIER 01 Ultra-Performance

Forged Monoblock

Carved exclusively from 10,000-ton forged 6061-T6 aerospace aluminum billets. Monolithic grain flow yields unmatched tensile strength and near-zero porosity.

Material:6061-T6 Billet
Grain structure:Continuous Radial
Ideal use:Hypercar / GT3 Cup
TIER 02 Track & Road

Hybrid Flow-Formed

Cast wheel centers dynamically heated and spun while high-pressure hydraulic rollers compress and elongate the barrel for severe curb impact absorption.

Material:Flow-Elongated A356
Shock absorption:+35% vs Cast
Ideal use:Sport Sedans / Daily
TIER 03 Bespoke Tailored

3-Piece Modular

Forged centers fastened to precision-spun inner barrels and stepped outer lips using titanium hardware. Allows sub-millimeter custom offsets and aggressive dish.

Fasteners:Titanium M8 Torx
Offset range:ET -40mm to +65mm
Ideal use:Widebody Commissions
§ 06 // Calder protocol

The process.

Six steps from raw billet to precision fitment
01
Specify & measure

Laser scanning hub, scrub radius, and caliper envelopes down to 0.05mm precision.

02
CAD & FEA Sim

Finite element stress analysis simulating curb impacts and 2.5G cornering loads.

03
CNC Milling

Robotic multi-axis milling carve spoke profiles and back-pad pocketing for un-sprung mass.

04
Hand Finishing

Hand brushwork followed by electro-deposited ceramic powder or PVD titanium coat.

05
Aerospace QC

X-ray density inspection, dynamic acoustic runout balancing, and TÜV spec sign-off.

06
Bespoke Crating

Hermetically sealed in tailored flight crates with individual serial certification plaque.
