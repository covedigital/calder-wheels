## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the calculated precision of bespoke automotive engineering merged with high-fashion editorial restraint. It targets discerning collectors, performance automotive connoisseurs, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals commissioning bespoke forged wheel fitments for exotic, classic, and hypercar platforms.

The experience evokes quiet power, uncompromising craftsmanship, and industrial sophistication. Visual rhythm is derived from museum-grade editorial catalogs and aeronautical telemetry displays rather than conventional commercial e-commerce.

The design movement is **Architectural High-End Minimalist with Precision Technicality**:
- Razor-sharp structural geometry with absolute zero-radius execution.
- Structural hairline borders (1px) evoking CNC wire EDM cuts and precision machining tolerances.
- Editorial narrative pacing: prominent alphanumeric indexing (`01 // SPECIFICATION`, `02 // FORGED ARCHITECTURE`), dramatic contrast shifts, and vast negative space balancing hyper-detailed mechanical schematics.
- Monochromatic baseline elevated by restrained champagne titanium and silver metallic accents.

## Colors

The palette operates in a controlled dark space reminiscent of a bespoke design atelier or a darkroom showcasing milled aerospace-grade 6061-T6 aluminum.

### Core Palettes
- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Primary canvas `#0A0A0B` (Near Black), elevated tier `#121214` (Charcoal), and nested surface tier `#1A1A1E` (Graphite).
- **Structural Hairlines:** `#26262B` provides the hairline boundary for grid cells, divider lines, and component containers. Hover and focus strokes step up to `#3A3A42`.
- **Primary Accent (Champagne Titanium):** `#C5A059` used sparingly for technical highlights, active fitment tolerances, bespoke commission status tags, and selected states.
- **Secondary Accent (Liquid Silver):** `#E5E5EA` applied to primary actions, prominent numerals, and focal interactive states.
- **Typography & Content:** `#F4F4F5` (Warm Off-White) for high-contrast editorial headlines, `#8E8E93` (Muted Technical Gray) for descriptive specifications, data keys, and metadata indices.

### Application Rules
Never deploy vibrant saturated hues for validation or status. Status is communicated through muted technical indicators: amber titanium for active/in-progress, subdued platinum for verified fitment, and deep oxidized slate for disabled/unavailable components.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial tension between sculptural, wide geometric sans display letterforms and cold, monospaced computational metadata.

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Industrial, geometric, and uncompromising. Rendered in tighter tracking at large scales to project machine-pressed authority. All section titles adopt uppercase formatting with leading editorial index markers (`01 //`, `02 //`).
- **Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Clean, understated Swiss-grotesque discipline ensuring effortless legibility when describing metallurgical compositions, offset engineering, and structural load ratings.
- **Labels & Spec Callouts (`JetBrains Mono`):** Meticulous monospace typesetting used for PCD bolts patterns, center-bore tolerances, ET offsets, pricing, and fitment telemetry. Always uppercase with generous letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout implements a structural 12-column architectural grid defined by crisp 1px borders rather than empty whitespace gaps. Layout containers visually fuse edge-to-edge, forming an engineering matrix.

### Structural Rules
- **Column Composition:** 12 columns on desktop (1440px+ max-width constrained canvas or edge-to-edge full bleed viewport with locked inset margins). 6 columns on tablet (768px - 1023px), 4 columns on mobile (&lt;768px).
- **Internal Cell Division:** Cells abut directly via `1px solid #26262B` dividing borders. Avoid floating "cards with margins" in main catalog and visualizer flows; construct contiguous data grids.
- **Rhythm & Insets:** Internal component padding strictly observes 8-point geometric intervals (`16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`).
- **Section Headers:** Every major viewport module begins with an editorial header bar anchored by an index coordinate (`01 // ARCHITECTURE`, `02 // AERODYNAMICS`) positioned left, and a system timestamp or status marker (`STATUS // SPECIFICATION READY`) aligned right.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects conventional dropped blur shadows in favor of **structural boundary elevation, planar shifts, and surgical luminescence**.

1. **Planar Tiers (Tonal Layering):**
   - **Ground (`#0A0A0B`):** Main viewport background, visualizer canvas, negative space.
   - **Tier 1 Surface (`#121214`):** Data tables, configuration sidebars, editorial panels.
   - **Tier 2 Surface (`#1A1A1E`):** Micro-tooltips, active selectors, modal fitment overlays.

2. **Boundary Definition:**
   - Depth is articulated strictly through `1px` hairlines (`#26262B`). When a surface is elevated or focused, its hairline boundary transitions to `#E5E5EA` or `#C5A059`, asserting crisp optical separation.

3. **Machined Glow (Specular Focus):**
   - Interactive nodes (e.g., active focal points on an interactive wheel 3D viewport) cast an ultra-concentrated titanium radial glow: `0 0 16px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **orthogonal, razor-sharp, and unyielding (0px border-radius)**. 

- All buttons, inputs, dialogs, media framing containers, tags, and viewport panels are built with square, 90-degree corners.
- Round geometry is exclusively reserved for literal wheel center caps, bolt patterns, and automotive cross-section schematics where technical geometry demands true circular representation.
- Inset chamfered accents (45-degree micro-notches, 3px by 3px) may be applied to active state indicators and corner coordinates to emphasize CNC-milled tooling cues.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#E5E5EA` background, `#0A0A0B` uppercase monospaced text (`11px`, `0.12em` tracking), `0px` radius, `1px solid #E5E5EA`. On hover, background shifts to `#C5A059` with border matching `#C5A059`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background transparent, text `#F4F4F5`, `1px solid #26262B`. On hover, border becomes `#E5E5EA` and background becomes `#121214`.
- **Technical Action / Commission:** Height `48px`, inline flex with an index prefix: `[ + ] INITIATE COMMISSION`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#121214`, border `1px solid #26262B`, text `#F4F4F5`, placeholder `#8E8E93`. Zero rounded corners. Focus shifts border to `#C5A059` with zero ambient halo.
- **Fitment Numeric Step Adjusters:** Custom monospaced stepped inputs (e.g., Offset `ET +32mm`) featuring sharp split left/right tactile increment blocks bordered by hairline dividers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Specification Badges:** Background `#1A1A1E`, border `1px solid #26262B`, text `#8E8E93` in `label-micro` typography.
- **Active State:** Background `#1A1A1E`, border `1px solid #C5A059`, text `#C5A059` with an optional leading bullet indicator (`●`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp 14x14px square boxes. Background `#0A0A0B`, border `1px solid #26262B`. Checked state fills with `#C5A059` displaying an inner 4x4px solid black square core.
- **Segmented Fitment Control:** Edge-to-edge joined horizontal tabs. Inactive tabs: `#121214` with `#8E8E93` text. Active tab: `#1A1A1E` with `#F4F4F5` text, topped by a 2px horizontal `#C5A059` hairline accent.

### Cards (Product & Spec Display)
- Contiguous cell structure. No floating drop shadows.
- Border `1px solid #26262B` encapsulating high-definition wheel imagery staged against deep monochromatic carbon gradients.
- Header row contains model designation (e.g., `CL-01 MONOBLOCK`) left-aligned, and wheel series category (`01 / TRACK READY`) right-aligned in `index-editorial`.
- Footer row houses an integrated data-strip displaying lug pattern, weight in kilograms, and forged composition.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Vehicle Telemetry Fitment Matrix:** Two-axis comparison grid mapping wheel diameter (19"-23"), width (8.5J-13J), and custom offsets against front/rear axle fender clearance thresholds.
- **Bespoke Finish Selector:** Square swatch tiles representing actual physical materials (Brushed Champagne Titanium, Liquid Silver, Satin Carbon, Dark Anodized Graphite) bordered in hairline metal strokes with live specular reflectivity highlights.
- **Machined Spec Callout:** Pointer overlay pins for 3D visualizers composed of a 1px vertical hairline, a horizontal tick line, and an anchored monospace data HUD (`OFFSET // -18MM NEGATIVE CAMBER`).
