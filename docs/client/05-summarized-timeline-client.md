# Calder Wheels — Project Timeline Summary

**For**: Ray Shadravan, Calder Wheels team
**From**: Cove Digital
**Start date**: August 31, 2026
**Estimated completion**: Mid-November 2026

---

## The Short Version

| Phase | What Happens | When |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **1. Planning & Design** | We map everything out — how the site works, how the configurator works, how the systems talk to each other. You approve the designs before we build. | **Weeks 1–2** (Aug 31 – Sep 13) |
| **2. Building the Store** | Your Shopify store takes shape — homepage, catalog, product pages, dealer portal foundation. | **Weeks 3–4** (Sep 14 – Sep 27) |
| **3. Configurator & Inventory** | The wheel builder goes live in staging. You'll be able to select finishes, see the wheel change on screen, and check pricing. The inventory logic (BOM) gets wired in. | **Weeks 4–6** (Sep 28 – Oct 11) |
| **4. QuickBooks & Shipping** | Orders flow automatically into QuickBooks for accounting and ShipStation for labels and tracking. | **Weeks 6–8** (Oct 5 – Oct 18) |
| **5. Automation & Testing** | Everything gets connected end-to-end. We test every path — retail order, dealer order, production status, inventory deduction. | **Weeks 8–10** (Oct 19 – Nov 1) |
| **6. Dealer Beta & Launch** | Dealers get access to test. We make final adjustments based on real use. Then we flip the switch. | **Weeks 10–12** (Nov 2 – Nov 15) |

## What This Timeline Doesn't Include (Phase 2)

These items are identified for a future phase and are not part of the current timeline:

- Vehicle-on-car visualizer (see the wheel on your actual car)
- Dealer sales commissions/spiffs
- Full CRM or loyalty program
- AI sales assistant

If any of these become priorities, we'll scope them separately.
