2. Scope of Work

The Provider will deliver a custom e-commerce and integration platform for Calder Wheels. The scope comprises two workstreams.

Workstream 1 — Commerce & Dealer Platform $11,750

  • Shopify Storefront — Premium, photography-led, dark/luxury Calder-branded store. Homepage, wheel catalog, product pages, galleries, responsive mobile layout, customer accounts, checkout, SEO/AEO and analytics foundation.
  • Wheel Configurator — 2D layered-image configurator: center finish × barrel/lip finish × hardware finish. Live visual preview, real-time price updating. Up to 5 initial wheel designs and 60 configuration combinations. Availability gate tied to Bill-of-Materials component stock. Vehicle-on-car visualizer excluded (Phase 2).
  • Retail Ordering — Standard DTC flow: configure → price → cart → payment → order.
  • Vehicle Fitment Finder — Year → Make → Model → Generation/Trim → compatible wheels. Never surface incompatible products. Manual curated database (ACES/PIES not included).
  • Dealer / Wholesale Portal — Dealer login and approved accounts, tiered pricing, dealer-only products, MOQ enforcement, payment terms (Net-30, prepaid/card), order history and tracking, abstracted production status. Dashboard, Quick Order, Quote Management, Dealer Catalog, Application Form screens.

Phase 2 Exclusions: Vehicle-on-car visualizer, dealer commission/spiff engine, advanced loyalty/rewards, full CRM, AI sales assistant, large-scale rendering production.

Workstream 2 — Systems Integration & Automation $4,250

  • QuickBooks Integration Service — Web Connector setup, qbXML communication, authentication, queue management. Customer sync (Shopify → QuickBooks with duplicate protection).
  • Accounting Transaction Creation — Order → QuickBooks invoice with customer, line items, SKU/item references, quantities, pricing, shipping, tax, and Shopify order reference.
  • BOM/Component Mapping — Customer configuration decomposed into component recipe; BOM drives component deduction in QuickBooks.
  • ShipStation Integration — Eligible order → ShipStation → label, tracking number, carrier, service pushed back to Shopify.
  • Integration Reliability — Idempotency, duplicate prevention, retry logic, integration logs, error reporting, failed-job queue, exception handling.

Environment: Integration with Intuit QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise Solutions 24.0 (Windows) via QuickBooks Web Connector and qbXML SDK.

3. Architecture & System Boundaries

The integration layer (n8n + custom ledger service) acts as the single orchestrator. A custom component ledger is the single source of truth for component quantities. Shopify and QuickBooks reflect this ledger.

SystemRoleOwns
ShopifyCommerce system of recordStorefront, products, configurator, checkout, payments, customer/dealer portals
QuickBooks Enterprise 24Financial/inventory system of recordAccounting customers, invoices, items, financial inventory, COGS, payments
ShipStationShipping system of recordShipments, carriers, services, labels, tracking
n8n / Cove layerOrchestrationData movement, BOM logic, available-to-build engine, workflow triggers, status translation, retry, exception routing

No system independently decides what another system owns. The architecture supports adding a Production & Fulfillment System (Workstream 3) later with zero redesign.

7. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to provide or arrange the following:

  1. Product Renderings: Standardized product renderings for up to 5 initial wheel designs. The Provider will prepare and configure these for the configurator. Extensive photorealistic rendering libraries are separately scoped.
  2. QuickBooks Environment: Administrative access to a properly licensed and operational QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise Solutions 24.0 Windows environment and company file.
  3. Third-Party Subscriptions: Shopify plan (Plus or Advanced recommended), QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise 24.0 license, ShipStation account, n8n plan, and any apps/themes/services required for the platform.
  4. Product Data: Wheel designs, finishes, SKUs, pricing (retail and dealer tier), fitment specifications, and component inventory data.
  5. Brand Assets: Logo, brand colors, fonts, and existing brand guidelines.
  6. Access: Appropriate access to Shopify admin, QuickBooks environment, domain/DNS management, and relevant systems.
  7. Timely Review: Timely feedback and approvals on deliverables to keep the project on schedule.

8. Scope Protections

1. QuickBooks Environment Assumption. The quoted integration assumes Calder provides administrative access to a properly licensed and operational QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise Solutions 24.0 environment. Windows server/hosting migration, QuickBooks repair, company-file remediation, connector replacement, network remediation, and data cleanup are outside scope.
2. Rendering Allowance. Calder supplies standardized product renderings (up to 5 designs, 60 combinations). Extensive photorealistic rendering libraries are separately scoped.
3. Third-Party Platform Changes. If Shopify, QuickBooks, ShipStation, or n8n significantly change their APIs, pricing, or capabilities during the project, scope and timeline may need adjustment. The Provider will notify the Client promptly.
4. Fitment Data. The fitment finder uses a manual curated database. ACES/PIES data licensing and integration is not included.
