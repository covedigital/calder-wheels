Before we finalize the Product Page and Build Your Wheel configurator, I'd like to confirm that the following functionality matches how you envision customers shopping for and customizing Calder Wheels.

The goal is to offer customers **preconfigured wheel designs that are ready to shop**, while also allowing them to customize those designs before adding them to their cart.

### 1. Product Listings — Preconfigured Wheel Designs

Customers will browse a catalog of preconfigured Calder wheel designs.

Each listing will feature a specific wheel design with predetermined finishes and hardware, including:

- Wheel design/model (e.g., CLB07)
- Diameter (22", 24", or 26")
- Center Finish (Gloss Black, Black Tint, or Titanium Tint)
- Barrel & Lip Finish (Black or Chrome)
- Hardware & Rivet Finish (Black Bolts or Chrome Bolts)

Each preconfigured design will have its own product listing, images, specifications, and starting configuration.

**Expected behavior:** Customers can purchase the displayed configuration or customize it further.



### 2. Product Page — Standard Wheel Selection

When customers open a product page, they will see the selected wheel design, images, specifications, pricing, fitment information, and purchase options.

Customers can adjust the following options directly on the Product Page:

| Option | Available selections |
|---|---|
| Diameter | 22", 24", 26" |
| Center Finish | Gloss Black, Black Tint, Titanium Tint |
| Barrel & Lip Finish | Black, Chrome |
| Hardware & Rivet Finish | Black Bolts, Chrome Bolts |

As customers change their selections:

- The selected configuration updates.
- The product price updates when applicable.
- The product's availability is checked.
- Product images update where matching imagery is available.
- The selected options remain consistent with the Build Your Wheel configurator.

**Expected behavior:** Customers can customize the product directly on the Product Page without necessarily using the full configurator.


===


### 3. Build Your Wheel — Visual Wheel Customization

The Product Page will include a dedicated **Build Your Wheel** section for customers who want a more interactive customization experience.

Customers will be able to:

1. Select the wheel diameter.
2. Choose the center finish.
3. Choose the barrel and lip finish.
4. Choose the hardware and rivet finish.
5. View the wheel preview as finishes change.
6. Review their selected configuration, price, and availability.
7. Add the customized wheel to their cart.

The configurator will use the wheel design from the current product as its starting point.

**Expected behavior:** Customers can customize the appearance of the wheel without having to find another preconfigured product listing.

====

### 4. Product Page & Configurator Synchronization

The standard Product Page options and Build Your Wheel configurator will remain synchronized.

For example:

If a customer changes the diameter from 22" to 24" on the Product Page, the configurator will also reflect the 24" selection.

Likewise, if a customer changes the Center Finish to Titanium Tint in the configurator, the Product Page will reflect that selection.

**Expected behavior:** Both interfaces always represent the same current wheel configuration, regardless of where the customer makes a change.

====

### 5. Pricing & Availability

Pricing will be based on the customer's selected wheel configuration.

- Diameter and finish selections may affect pricing based on the configured product prices.
- Black Bolts and Chrome Bolts will have the same price.
- The displayed price will reflect the selected configuration.
- Unavailable or unsupported combinations cannot be added to the cart.
- Made-to-order availability and estimated production lead times will be communicated where applicable.

**Expected behavior:** Customers see the correct price and purchasing availability before adding their wheel to the cart.

### 6. Add to Cart — Standard or Customized Wheel

Customers will have two ways to add a wheel to their cart:

**Option A — Product Page Add to Cart**

Adds the configuration currently selected on the Product Page.

**Option B — Build Your Wheel Add to Cart**

Adds the configuration currently selected in the configurator.

Both buttons will use the customer's latest selections, including hardware finish.

For example, a customer opens a product originally configured as:

- Diameter: 22"
- Center Finish: Gloss Black
- Barrel & Lip Finish: Black
- Hardware: Black Bolts

The customer then changes the configuration to:

- Diameter: 26"
- Center Finish: Titanium Tint
- Barrel & Lip Finish: Chrome
- Hardware: Chrome Bolts

When they click Add to Cart, **the cart should contain the customized 26" Titanium Tint / Chrome / Chrome Bolts configuration**, not the original preconfigured wheel.

### 7. Shopping Cart & Order Details

The cart and order should preserve the customer's complete wheel configuration.

Each wheel should include:

- Wheel design/model
- Diameter
- Center Finish
- Barrel & Lip Finish
- Hardware & Rivet Finish
- Quantity
- Final price

These details should remain available for order processing and manufacturing.

### 8. Standalone Build Your Wheel Page

In addition to the configurator on individual Product Pages, Calder will have a standalone Build Your Wheel page.

Customers will be able to:

- Choose a Calder wheel design.
- Customize all four options.
- View the resulting configuration.
- Review price and availability.
- Add their customized wheel to the cart.

The standalone builder will follow the same configuration, pricing, and purchasing rules as the Product Page configurator.
